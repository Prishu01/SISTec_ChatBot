## CHUNK 7: ADMISSION DETAILS — B.TECH

**Eligibility:**
- Passed 10+2 in Science stream (PCM/PCB) from a recognized board.
- Minimum 50% for General category, 45% for SC/ST/OBC.
- For Other State candidates: 45% for General and SC/ST both.

**Admission Basis:**
- JEE Mains Score (Priority) — for 1st year admission.
- 12th Result Based — for 1st year direct admission.

**Admission Process:**
1. Online Counseling via DTE, Bhopal (MP DTE — 2 rounds based on JEE Score).
2. College Level Counseling (CLC) after DTE rounds.
3. Document Verification.
4. Online Preference / Choice Filling.
5. Part Payment of Tuition Fee.
6. Online Allotment.

**Documents Required:**
- JEE Mains Scorecard, 10th Mark Sheet, 12th Mark Sheet, Transfer Certificate, Migration Certificate, Character Certificate, Caste Certificate (if applicable), Gap Certificate (if applicable), Income Certificate (if applicable), Domicile Certificate (MP candidates), Aadhar Card, 3 passport-size photographs.

**Note:** Only TC and Migration Certificate need to be submitted permanently. All other documents are returned after verification.

## CHUNK 21: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q. What is the accreditation of SISTec?**
A. SISTec is accredited by NAAC. Additionally, CSE, EX, ECE, and ME departments are accredited by NBA.

**Q. Is there a dress code at SISTec?**
A. No, there is no dress code at SISTec, but students are advised to wear decent and professional outfits.

**Q. Is hostel facility available?**
A. Yes, separate hostels for boys and girls are available within the campus. Mess facility is also available for hostellers and day-scholars.

**Q. Is transport facility available?**
A. Yes, a fleet of GPS and CCTV-enabled buses covers all major corners of Bhopal.

**Q. Can admission be cancelled?**
A. Yes, for detailed process visit the DTE MP website.

**Q. Is physical presence required during admission?**
A. Yes, physical presence is required during the allotment process.

**Q. Does SISTec help in getting education loans?**
A. The institute does not directly arrange loans but provides all necessary documents for loan processing.

**Q. What if a student scores 90%+ in 12th?**
A. Students scoring 90%+ in 12th exams are entitled to certain benefits and privileges. Contact the campus for details.

**Q. Which university is SISTec affiliated to?**
A. Engineering and Pharmacy — RGPV. Management (BBA/MBA) — Barkatullah University (BU Bhopal).

**Q. How many colleges are there in SGI?**
A. 6 colleges: SISTec, SISTec-R, SISTec-E, SIPTec, SIPTec-R, and SISTec Business School.
